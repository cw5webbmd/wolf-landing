What’s Mine, What’s Yours, What’s Ours

Every asset in your household belongs in one of three buckets: His, Hers, or Ours. How those assets are titled (and whether they sit inside a trust) determines where they actually go if something happens to you.

His

Individually owned assets

  • 🏦 Checking account (in his name only)
  • 💳 Personal credit card
  • 🚗 Car titled only to him
  • ⌚ Watch or hobby collection

Ours

Joint or marital assets

  • 🏠 Family home (titled jointly)
  • 🏦 Joint savings or checking account
  • 🚙 Shared family vehicle
  • 📈 Joint investment or brokerage account

Without clear titling, trusts, and beneficiary designations, these “ours” assets may not automatically go where you expect. State law and the probate court decide what happens when there is no estate plan.

Hers

Individually owned assets

  • 🏦 Checking account (in her name only)
  • 💳 Personal credit card
  • 👜 Handbags, jewelry, or luxury items
  • 🏍️ Motorcycle or vehicle titled only to her

Key idea: Ownership and titling decide where assets go by default. WOLF helps you move assets from “who owns it now” to “who should receive it later” through coordinated trusts, wills, and beneficiary designations—under your rules, not the state’s.
